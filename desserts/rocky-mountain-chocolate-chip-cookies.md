# Rocky Mountain Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Source:** https://isinginthekitchen.com/2016/05/01/silver-palate-rocky-mountain-chocolate-chip-cookies-new-music-from-bows/
**Originally from:** The Silver Palate Good Times Cookbook
**Makes:** About 100 small or 50 large cookies | **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)

## Ingredients
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups quick-cooking oats
- 12 ounces semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup coarsely chopped walnuts

## Steps
1. Cream the butter with both sugars until light and fluffy. Beat in the eggs, milk, and vanilla.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Add to the wet mixture and mix just until combined.
3. Stir in the oats, then fold in the chocolate chips and walnuts.
4. Refrigerate the dough for at least 1 hour.
5. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line baking sheets with parchment.
6. Shape the dough into balls (a rounded teaspoon for small cookies, a scant tablespoon for large). Press each one down a little and place them 2 inches apart.
7. Bake 8–10 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the centers still look pale.
8. Cool on the baking sheets for 5 minutes, then move to wire racks to finish cooling.
