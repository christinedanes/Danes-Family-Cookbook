# Thick and Chewy M&M Cookie Bars

**Source:** https://www.whatmegansmaking.com/2013/01/thick-and-chewy-mm-cookie-bars.html
**Originally from:** *Fantastic Family Favorites*
**Makes:** One 9x13 pan | **Time:** 45 minutes | **Oven:** 325°F

## Ingredients
- 2 1/8 cups flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 12 tablespoons (1 1/2 sticks) butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 (12 oz) bag M&M's, divided

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 325°F with a rack in the lower-middle position. Grease a 9x13 pan.
2. Whisk the flour, salt, and baking soda in a medium bowl.
3. In a large bowl, whisk the melted butter with both sugars, then mix in the egg, yolk, and vanilla.
4. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet with a spatula, just until combined. Don't overmix.
5. Fold in 1 cup of the M&M's. Spread the dough in the pan and smooth the top, then scatter the rest of the M&M's over it and press them in lightly.
6. Bake 25–30 minutes, until the top is light golden and a bit firm and the edges pull away from the pan.
7. Let cool completely before cutting.

## Notes
- No mixer needed.
- Swap the M&M's for chocolate chips or Reese's Pieces.
- In a 9x13 pan the bars come out a little thinner; the author has also made them in a 9x9 pan.
