# Halloumi, Cuke & Walnut Spoon Salad

**Originally from:** *More Is More* by Molly Baz
**Serves:** 4

## Ingredients
- 3 Persian cucumbers, chopped
- 2 small bunches tender herbs (dill, parsley, and/or mint), chopped
- 4 scallions, sliced
- 3 baby dill pickles (or 1 regular dill pickle), chopped
- 1/2 cup pickled jalapeños, chopped, plus 1/2 cup of their brine
- 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 8 ounces halloumi cheese, diced
- 3/4 cup walnuts, toasted
- Toasted cumin seeds (optional)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Steps
1. Toss the cucumbers, herbs, scallions, pickles, and jalapeños together in a large bowl.
2. Add the olive oil and jalapeño brine and toss again. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Brown the halloumi in a skillet over medium-high heat until golden and a little melty, then add it to the bowl.
4. Top with the walnuts and cumin seeds (if using), stir gently, and serve with spoons.

## Notes
- Chop everything small. It's meant to be eaten with a spoon.
