# 30 Minute Honey Garlic Chicken

**Source:** https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/honey-garlic-chicken/
**Originally from:** Tieghan Gerard (Half Baked Harvest)
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 30 minutes (10 prep + 20 cook) | **Oven:** 450°F

## Ingredients
### Chicken and Vegetables
- 2 lb chicken breasts or thighs, cut into cubes
- 1 Tbsp garlic powder
- Black pepper and chile flakes, to taste
- ¼ cup flour or gluten-free flour
- 4 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil, divided
- 3 cups broccoli florets or chopped asparagus
- Salt, for the vegetables
- Sesame seeds, for serving

### Honey Garlic Sauce
- ⅓ cup tamari or soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar (the original uses lemon juice)
- 3 Tbsp honey
- 6 cloves garlic, grated
- 3–4 Tbsp chili paste, such as gochujang
- 2 Tbsp chopped green onion, plus more for serving
- 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil (optional)

## Steps
1. **Heat the oven:** Preheat to 450°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment or rub it with oil.
2. **Coat the chicken:** On the sheet pan, toss the chicken with the garlic powder, pepper, and chile flakes. Add the flour and toss again, then add 2 Tbsp olive oil and toss to coat.
3. **Add the vegetables:** Push the chicken to one side of the pan. Put the broccoli or asparagus on the other side and toss with the rest of the olive oil, salt, and pepper.
4. **Bake:** Bake 10–15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
5. **Make the sauce:** While it bakes, stir all the sauce ingredients together in a bowl or jar.
6. **Sauce the chicken:** Take the vegetables off the pan. Pour the sauce over the chicken, toss, and bake 5 more minutes so the sauce sticks.
7. **Serve:** Top with green onions and sesame seeds. Great over rice, or on its own as an appetizer.

## Wing Version
For about 2 lb of chicken wings. Make the same sauce, and double it if you're cooking more than 2 lb.

1. **Dry:** Pat the wings very dry with paper towels. Dry skin is what makes them crispy.
2. **Season:** Toss with the garlic powder, pepper, chile flakes, a little salt, and 1 Tbsp baking powder (not baking soda). Skip the flour and oil.
3. **Arrange:** Set the wings in one layer on a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan. No rack? Use parchment and flip them halfway.
4. **Bake:** Bake at 425°F for 40–50 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp. Wings are best a little past done, around 175–190°F inside.
5. **Thicken the sauce:** While they bake, simmer the sauce in a small pan for 3–5 minutes until it thickens a bit, so it clings to the wings.
6. **Toss and glaze:** Toss the hot wings in the sauce, put them back on the pan, and bake 5 more minutes, or broil 2–3 minutes for a sticky glaze. Watch closely, because honey burns fast.
7. **Serve:** Top with sesame seeds and green onions.

## Notes
- Rice vinegar replaces the original lemon juice. Lime juice also works 1:1. Apple cider vinegar works too, but start with about 1½ Tbsp since it's sharper.
- The flour is a very light coating, just enough for the sauce to stick. It isn't breaded.
- Toasted sesame oil can be left out.
- The author likes Korean chile flakes and gochujang (Mother in Law's brand).
- If the sauce is still runny after baking, broil for 2 minutes.
- Chili paste and chile flakes add heat. Use less for kids or anyone sensitive to spice.
