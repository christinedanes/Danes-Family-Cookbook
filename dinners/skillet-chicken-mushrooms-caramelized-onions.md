# Skillet Chicken With Mushrooms and Caramelized Onions

**Source:** NYT Cooking, by Yasmin Fahr
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

### Marinade
- 2 tablespoons olive oil (plus 3 tablespoons more for cooking, below)
- 2 tablespoons sherry vinegar (plus 2 teaspoons more to finish, below)
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1/4 teaspoon red-pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for seasoning
- Black pepper

### Skillet
- 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3-inch pieces
- 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced (about 4 cups)
- 3/4 pound cremini mushrooms, stems removed, thinly sliced (about 4 cups)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons sherry vinegar

### To Finish
- 1/2 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley or dill, roughly chopped
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan or pecorino (optional)
- Crusty bread or cooked pasta, for serving

## Steps
1. **Marinate:** Whisk 2 Tbsp oil, 2 Tbsp vinegar, the honey, mustard, pepper flakes, and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl. Pat the chicken dry, season with salt and pepper, and toss it in the marinade. Let it sit at room temperature while you cook the vegetables, stirring once.
2. **Onions:** Heat a 12-inch cast-iron or heavy skillet over medium-high until very hot (1 1/2–2 minutes) — no oil yet. Spread the onions in an even layer, salt them, and cook about 4 minutes, stirring every minute or so.
3. **Mushrooms:** Add the mushrooms, salt them, and stir. The pan will look crowded; that's fine. Cook about 4 minutes, stirring now and then, until they shrink and start to brown.
4. **Oil:** Stir in the remaining 3 Tbsp oil and cook about 2 minutes more, until the onions start to color. Turn the heat down if anything starts to burn.
5. **Chicken:** Push the vegetables to the sides and put the chicken in the center. Pour any leftover marinade over the vegetables. Cook the chicken without moving it for 4–5 minutes, then stir everything together and cook about 10 minutes more, until the chicken is cooked through. Drop to medium if the onions get too dark.
6. **Finish:** Add the last 2 tsp vinegar and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Taste and add salt.
7. **Serve:** Take off the heat, top with the herbs and cheese (if using), and serve with bread or pasta.

## Notes
- The onions go into a dry, very hot pan on purpose. It makes them release their water and turn sweet quickly.
- For something green, stir in spinach at the end to wilt, or serve with a green salad or roasted broccoli.
- Shredded chicken over buttered noodles is a great way to serve it.
- Reader tips: try 1/4 cup wine instead of the sherry vinegar for a richer flavor, or use half the chicken with double the onions and mushrooms.
