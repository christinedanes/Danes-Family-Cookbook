# Sweet Potato Burger Bowls

**Source:** https://eatwithclarity.com/sweet-potato-burger-bowls/
**Originally from:** Claire Cary, Eat With Clarity
**Time:** 50 minutes (20 prep, 30 cook) | **Oven:** 400°F

## Ingredients

### Beef
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 Tbsp butter (optional)

### Sweet Potatoes
- 2 medium sweet potatoes
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika

### Bowl
- 4 cups shredded lettuce
- 1 cup grape or cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, sliced
- 3/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Pickles (optional)

### Special Sauce
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 Tbsp mustard
- 1 Tbsp ketchup
- 1 Tbsp sweet relish
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. Cube the sweet potatoes and toss with the olive oil and the sweet potato spices.
3. Spread on the sheet and roast about 30 minutes, until fork-tender.
4. Meanwhile, brown the beef in a skillet over medium heat. Stir in the beef spices (and butter, if using).
5. Whisk the sauce ingredients together.
6. Let the beef and potatoes cool a few minutes so they don't wilt the lettuce.
7. Build each bowl: lettuce, tomatoes, beef, sweet potatoes, red onion, cheese, and pickles if you like. Drizzle with sauce.

## Notes
- Ground turkey or chicken works instead of beef.
- Any mustard works; the original uses yellow.
- Wedges or fries work instead of cubes.
- About 602 calories and 27g protein per bowl (from the original page).
