# Naked Chicken Tenders

**Source:** https://sailorbailey.com/blog/chicken-tenders/
**Originally from:** Bailey Rhatigan (Sailor Bailey)
**Serves:** 2 | **Time:** About 25 minutes (5 prep + 18 cook) | **Air fryer:** 350°F | **Oven:** 375°F

## Ingredients
- 1 to 1½ lb chicken breast
- 1½ Tbsp cornstarch (optional, for a crispier coating)
- ½ tsp salt (use less if your Everything But The Bagel seasoning is salty)
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- ½ tsp mustard powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 to 2 tsp Everything But The Bagel seasoning (optional)
- ½ Tbsp olive oil, plus more as needed

## Steps
1. **Prep:** Pat the chicken dry and cut it into tender-sized pieces, about 2 inches long and wide.
2. **Season:** In a medium bowl, whisk the cornstarch with all the seasonings.
3. **Coat:** Add the oil and chicken and toss until every piece is coated.
4. **Air fryer:** Line the basket with a little parchment and spray it (optional). Lay the tenders in one layer, not touching. Cook at 350°F for 13–14 minutes, until the inside reaches 165°F.
5. **Or oven:** Lay the tenders flat on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 375°F for 10 minutes, flip, then bake 5–6 minutes more, until the inside reaches 165°F.
6. **Serve:** Let them cool a minute and serve with your favorite dipping sauce.

## Notes
- Store-bought chicken tenders work too, no cutting needed.
- Arrowroot powder can replace the cornstarch, and avocado oil can replace the olive oil.
- The air fryer makes them crispier; the oven makes them softer.
- Cayenne adds real heat. Leave it out for kids or anyone sensitive to spice.
- For a thicker, crispier coating, the author suggests ¼ to ⅓ cup cornstarch.
- Leftovers keep 4 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer. Thaw, then reheat in the air fryer or oven.
