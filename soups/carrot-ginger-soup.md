# Carrot-Ginger Soup

**From:** *Run Fast. Eat Slow.* by Shalane Flanagan and Elyse Kopecky
**Simmer time:** 35–40 minutes

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons butter or olive oil
- 2 yellow onions, sliced
- 1 1/2 lbs carrots, peeled and chopped
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 2 tablespoons uncooked rice (thickens the soup naturally)
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

**Optional toppings:** toasted pumpkin seeds, goat cheese, or plain Greek yogurt

## Steps
1. **Sauté:** Warm the butter or oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onions, carrots, and salt and cook about 8 minutes, until softened.
2. **Simmer:** Pour in the broth and add the rice. Bring to a boil, then cover, lower the heat, and simmer 35–40 minutes, until the carrots and rice are very tender.
3. **Blend:** Take the pot off the heat and let it cool 10–15 minutes. Blend until completely smooth, in a blender or with an immersion blender.
4. **Finish:** Stir in the lemon juice, ginger, and pepper.
5. **Serve:** Top with pumpkin seeds or a spoonful of Greek yogurt.
